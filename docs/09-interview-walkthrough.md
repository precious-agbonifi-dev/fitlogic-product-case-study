# Interview walkthrough — FitLogic

## 60-second project story
“I used my hands-on experience coaching fitness clients to frame a consumer product concept around workout consistency. I built a Lean Canvas, defined early-adopter personas and a clear value proposition, and translated that into an MVP PRD and prioritised roadmap. I also designed an event taxonomy, product metrics and SQL analyses so the product can be evaluated through behaviours rather than vanity metrics. It is a concept-stage independent project; the next step is to validate assumptions through interviews and prototype tests.”

## Why this matters to a fintech PM or analyst
The techniques transfer across regulated consumer products: clear customer segmentation, friction reduction, onboarding conversion, retention cohorts, trustworthy event data, and disciplined prioritisation.

## Questions to prepare for
**Why this customer problem?** Fitness coaching experience exposed repeated planning and consistency challenges; these are hypotheses to validate in wider research.

**Why not build social features first?** They add scope without proving the basic outcome: users repeatedly complete workouts.

**What would you measure first?** Onboarding completion, first workout within 7 days, weekly adherence and week-4 retention, with data-quality guardrails.

**What did you actually do?** Independently created the product strategy, documentation, event design and illustrative SQL. Do not imply a shipped app or validated outcomes.

**What would you change after testing?** Revise personas, onboarding requirements, metric thresholds and RICE assumptions based on evidence.

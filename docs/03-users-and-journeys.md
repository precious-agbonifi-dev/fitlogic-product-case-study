# Users and journeys

## Persona A — Time-poor beginner (hypothetical)
**Goal:** Build a sustainable 2–3 day routine.  
**Context:** Variable work hours; uncertain which exercises to choose.  
**Pain points:** Overly complex plans, low confidence, missed-session guilt.  
**Success:** Can begin the first session quickly and recover from a disrupted week.

## Persona B — Returning gym-goer (hypothetical)
**Goal:** Regain consistency while tracking strength and energy.  
**Context:** Has trained before but has lost a repeatable routine.  
**Pain points:** Generic programmes and progress that is hard to interpret.  
**Success:** Understands what to do next and sees improvement over weeks.

## Core journey
Discover → Sign up → Choose goal/days/experience → See week-one plan → Start workout → Mark complete → Review weekly progress → Adjust plan.

## Journey friction and proposed responses
| Step | Possible friction | Product response | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Too many questions | Three essential inputs | Completion rate |
| First session | Unclear next action | Prominent “Start workout” | First workout within 7 days |
| Week two | Missed workout | Non-judgemental rescheduling | Return rate |
| Progress | Hard to see change | Weekly adherence summary | Weekly engaged users |

## Accessibility and inclusion
Plain-language instructions, accessible contrast, screen-reader labels and alternative exercises should be considered in design testing.

Personas are provisional and require research validation.

# MVP prioritisation and proposed roadmap

## RICE scoring (illustrative assumptions)
RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort. Higher score indicates stronger relative priority. These are planning estimates, not empirical data.

| Feature | Reach | Impact | Confidence | Effort | RICE |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Goal-led onboarding | 100 | 2 | 0.8 | 3 | 53.3 |
| Track workout completion | 90 | 2 | 0.8 | 3 | 48.0 |
| Weekly progress dashboard | 80 | 1.5 | 0.7 | 4 | 21.0 |
| Reschedule missed workouts | 60 | 1.5 | 0.6 | 5 | 10.8 |
| Social challenges | 35 | 1 | 0.4 | 8 | 1.8 |

Reach is a hypothetical cohort count; impact and effort use relative planning scales.

## Now — prototype
- Map onboarding and first-workout flow.
- Test comprehension with target users.
- Define event names and analytics properties.

## Next — MVP delivery
- Build account and goal-led onboarding.
- Display curated plans and workout instructions.
- Record completion events and show weekly progress.

## Later — only after learning
- Flexible rescheduling.
- Personalised recommendations based on explicit preferences.
- Subscription tests and trainer tools.

## Milestones
| Phase | Illustrative duration | Exit criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | 2 weeks | Key problem and target segment evidenced |
| Prototype | 2 weeks | Core flow usable in tests |
| Build | 4–6 weeks | Acceptance criteria met |
| Pilot | 2–4 weeks | Reliable measurement and actionable feedback |

Dates are a proposed delivery plan, not completed milestones.

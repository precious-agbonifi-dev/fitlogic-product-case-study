# Product positioning

## Target
Busy adults starting or restarting a fitness routine who want structure without complex planning.

## Category
Consumer fitness planning and progress-tracking app.

## Positioning statement
**For** time-poor fitness beginners **who** find it difficult to maintain a consistent training routine, **FitLogic** is a goal-led fitness planning app **that** turns available time into a practical weekly training plan and makes adherence visible, **unlike** unstructured video libraries or manual workout notes.

## Messaging pillars
1. **Clarity:** Know what to do today.
2. **Flexibility:** Build around the week you actually have.
3. **Progress:** See consistency before chasing perfection.

## Alternatives to test
| Alternative | Strength | Potential unmet need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free video libraries | Accessible exercise variety | Personal progression and continuity |
| Manual notes/spreadsheets | Flexible and low cost | Guided planning and feedback |
| Broad fitness apps | Rich tracking | Simplicity for new users |
| One-to-one coaching | Personal attention | Affordability and on-demand access |

No comparative performance or market-share claims are made without research.

## Pricing assumption
Test free basic tracking versus paid adaptive programming. Avoid setting final prices before interviews and willingness-to-pay research.

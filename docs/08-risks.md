# Product risk register

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Proposed mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product advice mistaken for medical advice | High | Medium | Safety messaging, qualified review and clear limitations |
| Sensitive information collected unnecessarily | High | Medium | Data minimisation and privacy review |
| Poor onboarding retention | Medium | High | Prototype and funnel testing |
| Low-quality activity tracking | High | Medium | Event contracts, deduplication and QA |
| Overengineering before validation | Medium | High | Concierge pilot and MVP scope |
| Accessibility barriers | High | Medium | WCAG-informed design and usability testing |
| Subscription proposition unvalidated | Medium | High | Willingness-to-pay tests |

## Decision log
**Decision:** Prioritise initial workout completion over gamification.  
**Rationale:** The key assumption is that consistency is the main customer problem; test this before feature expansion.  
**Evidence status:** Unvalidated hypothesis.

## Governance
Require a privacy assessment before collecting personal data, secure storage design, explicit consent where necessary, and an appropriate retention/deletion policy.

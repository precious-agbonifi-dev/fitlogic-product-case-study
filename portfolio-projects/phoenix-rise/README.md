# Phoenix Rise

A mobile-first, cycle-optional women’s movement and wellbeing prototype inspired by the Phoenix Rise concept and supplied screen.

## Open the prototype

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build tools or dependencies are required.

## What works

- Daily mood and energy check-ins, with optional symptoms and cycle day.
- Cycle tracking can be enabled or switched off; prototype data is stored in browser `localStorage`.
- Adaptable starter plans for strength, running, swimming and recovery.
- A guided breathing pause, affirmations, and wellbeing information.
- Topic-filtered community demo posts and locally saved new posts.
- Responsive navigation for desktop and mobile.

## Prototype limits

This is a front-end demonstration. It has no sign-in, server, multi-user community, moderation service, health-device integration, clinical review workflow, or production privacy controls. Demo data is stored on the current browser/device. Avoid entering personal or sensitive information. Content is general wellbeing information and is not a diagnosis or treatment plan.

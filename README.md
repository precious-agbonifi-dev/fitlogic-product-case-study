## Executive summary
FitLogic explores a single place for busy fitness beginners to discover structured workouts, follow progressive plans and understand their progress. The opportunity is to make programme adherence easier without overwhelming people with features.

**Product problem:** Fitness beginners often find it hard to translate ambitious goals into achievable weekly routines, know whether they are making progress, or maintain accountability.

**Proposed value proposition:** A simple, goal-led plan with visible progress and supportive weekly check-ins.

**North-star candidate:** Weekly active users completing at least two planned workouts. This is a hypothesis to validate, not a measured result.

## Product decisions
1. **Prioritise adherence over feature breadth:** A user must complete and repeat workouts before advanced social features will matter.
2. **Reduce onboarding friction:** Start with goal, available days and experience level, rather than lengthy questionnaires.
3. **Measure behaviour, not downloads:** Activation and 4-week retention are more useful than sign-up totals alone.
4. **Protect user trust:** Minimise sensitive data, clarify health-related limitations and make privacy controls discoverable.

## MVP hypothesis
For time-constrained beginners, a short onboarding and achievable weekly plan will increase 4-week adherence compared with an unstructured workout library. This remains untested.

## Method and limitations
The concept draws on practical personal-training domain knowledge and self-directed software and analytics learning. Competitive assumptions, pricing and customer needs require primary research. Example SQL queries use the proposed event model; they do not represent live product telemetry.

**Portfolio use:** Suitable for Associate Product Manager, Product Manager and Product Analyst applications in technology and fintech where user value, data literacy and prioritisation are relevant.
